Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 June 2026

Roomwise is currently a beta interior-design tool. This notice explains how the app handles room photos, design preferences, shopping selections, and support messages before the product is sold to customers.

Information the app handles

  • Room photos uploaded from your device or through the phone-capture link.
  • Room type, style, palette, budget, location, and notes about what to keep or avoid.
  • Selected products and shopping-list actions saved locally in your browser.
  • Basic technical data such as request paths and status codes recorded by Vercel runtime logs.

How room photos are used

Room photos are used to preview your room, transfer a photo from phone to desktop, and, when AI rendering is enabled, request a redesigned after image. Do not upload photos containing people, private documents, valuables, or anything you do not want processed for a design preview.

Local storage

Roomwise saves lightweight workspace state in your browser so preferences and shopping picks can survive a page refresh. This includes design preferences and selected product IDs, not full room photos. You can clear this state with the Reset workspace control or through your browser settings.

AI processing

When live rendering is enabled, Roomwise sends the room image and design brief to the configured AI image provider to generate an after render. In demo mode, AI rendering is disabled and the app shows a demo notice.

Shopping links

Shopping recommendations are provided to help you recreate a room concept. Product availability, pricing, delivery, dimensions, and suitability must be checked with the retailer before purchase.

Data retention

The current beta uses short-lived in-memory phone-capture storage and local browser storage. Production account storage is not enabled yet. Before taking paying customers, Roomwise should add account controls for saved projects, deletion, and export.

Contact
