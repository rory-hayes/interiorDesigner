Beta terms

Last updated: 20 June 2026

Roomwise is a beta design-planning tool. These terms are a practical beta notice and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before the service is sold broadly.

What Roomwise provides

Roomwise helps create interior design concepts, AI-assisted room renders when enabled, and shopping-list ideas from a room photo, budget, style, palette, and user notes.

No professional advice

Roomwise does not provide architectural, structural, electrical, safety, or regulated interior design advice. Before making changes to a home, users should check measurements, access, safety, lease rules, installation requirements, and professional guidance where needed.

AI and visual accuracy

AI renders are concept previews. They may contain visual errors, inaccurate scale, unavailable items, incorrect materials, or unrealistic lighting. Users should treat renders as inspiration, not a final specification.

Shopping recommendations

Product names, prices, links, dimensions, and availability can change. Users are responsible for confirming all retailer details before purchase. Roomwise is not responsible for retailer stock, delivery, returns, warranties, installation, or product suitability.

User responsibilities

  • Only upload room photos you have the right to use.
  • Do not upload photos containing people, private documents, or sensitive personal information.
  • Check that any design change is safe, practical, and allowed in the property.
  • Confirm product measurements before buying.

Beta availability

The beta may change, fail, or be unavailable while features are being improved. Demo mode may disable live AI rendering until the production API key and supporting services are configured.
